Actionable guidelines

Using TikTok intentionally

The bigger picture

Rather than viewing TikTok as simply beneficial or harmful, it’s more useful to understand how students use it and how it fits into their everyday lives. For Multimedia Arts students, it can be both a creative resource and a source of distraction, depending on how intentionally they use it and whether they keep boundaries around their responsibilities.
